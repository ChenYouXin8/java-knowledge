# 精读 04 · Why Sleep Matters（睡眠为什么重要）

> 主题：睡眠与健康 | 难度：四级 | 约 215 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Meng used to sleep at one or two in the morning and wake up at nine. He thought he felt fine. But his grades were falling, and he could not remember things easily. His doctor said the problem was simple: he was not sleeping enough.

Sleep is not a waste of time. While you sleep, your brain is working hard. It cleans out waste, organizes memories, and prepares you for the next day. Scientists have found that people who sleep less than seven hours a night have more trouble focusing and learning new things.

Your body needs a regular schedule. Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day helps your body clock stay stable. Even on weekends, try not to sleep in too late.

What can you do tonight? Keep your bedroom dark and quiet. Put your phone away at least 30 minutes before bed, because the blue light from screens can keep you awake. Avoid coffee in the afternoon. And do not exercise too late in the evening.

You might think that sleeping less gives you more time. In fact, sleeping well gives you better time. With enough sleep, you can think faster, learn better, and feel happier. So tonight, close your eyes early. Your future self will thank you.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> Scientists have found that people who sleep less than seven hours a night have more trouble focusing and learning new things.

- 主干：Scientists have **found** that...（主 + 谓 + 宾语从句）
- 修饰：从句主干是 `people have more trouble`；`who sleep less than seven hours a night` 是**定语从句**修饰 people；`focusing and learning new things` 是 trouble 的后置说明（做某事有困难）
- 译文：科学家发现，每晚睡眠不足七小时的人，在专注和学习新事物上会遇到更多困难。

**句 2：**
> With enough sleep, you can think faster, learn better, and feel happier.

- 主干：you can **think** faster, **learn** better, and **feel** happier（三个并列谓语）
- 修饰：`With enough sleep` 是方式/条件状语（有了充足的睡眠）
- 译文：有了充足的睡眠，你能想得更快、学得更好、过得更开心。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. Why were Meng's grades falling?
2. What does the brain do while you sleep? Give two examples.
3. Why should we keep a regular sleep schedule?
4. What are two things you can do tonight to sleep better?
5. What is the difference between "sleeping less gives you more time" and "sleeping well gives you better time"?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. Because he was not sleeping enough (he slept late and woke late).
2. It cleans out waste and organizes memories.
3. Because a regular schedule helps your body clock stay stable.
4. Keep the bedroom dark and quiet; put the phone away before bed; avoid coffee; don't exercise too late (any two).
5. Less sleep does not really add useful time; good sleep makes the time you have better.

## 五、Summary 练习（第 3 个月阶段用，现在可以先看一眼）

**公式：谁 + 做了什么 + 结果/影响**

参考答案（3-5 句）：
> Meng felt fine but slept too little, and his grades fell. In fact, sleep is not wasted time: the brain cleans waste and organizes memories while we sleep. People who sleep less than seven hours have more trouble focusing. To sleep well, keep a regular schedule, make the bedroom dark and quiet, and avoid screens before bed. Good sleep helps us think faster and learn better.
