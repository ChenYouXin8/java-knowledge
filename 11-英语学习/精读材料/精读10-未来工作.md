# 精读 10 · Machines and the Future of Work（机器与工作的未来）

> 主题：AI 与未来工作 | 难度：四级 | 约 220 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Will robots take our jobs? This question makes many people worried. But experts say the future of work is not so simple. Machines will change many jobs, but they will also create new ones.

In the past, machines replaced people who did heavy physical work, such as carrying things in factories. Today, computers and AI are getting better at tasks that need thinking, like translating languages or checking data. Some jobs will indeed disappear.

However, history tells us that technology usually creates more jobs than it destroys. When cars appeared, horse-carriage drivers lost their jobs, but many new jobs appeared in driving, road building, and car repair. The same thing may happen with AI.

So what should young people do? First, learn to work with technology. AI is a tool, not a monster. People who can use it well will have an advantage. Second, develop skills that machines find hard: creativity, communication, and caring for others. These human skills will become more valuable.

Third, keep learning. The world is changing fast, and the skills you need today may be different tomorrow. Lifelong learning is not a slogan; it is a survival skill.

The future will not belong to machines or to humans alone. It will belong to those who can combine human hearts with machine power. So do not fear the future. Prepare for it.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> People who can use it well will have an advantage.

- 主干：People **will have** an advantage（主 + 谓 + 宾）
- 修饰：`who can use it well` 是**定语从句**修饰 People（能很好使用它的人）
- 译文：能很好使用它（AI）的人将会拥有优势。

**句 2：**
> It will belong to those who can combine human hearts with machine power.

- 主干：It **will belong to** those（主 + 谓 + 宾）
- 修饰：`who can combine human hearts with machine power` 是**定语从句**修饰 those（那些能把人心与机器力量结合起来的人）
- 译文：未来将属于那些能把人的心灵与机器的力量结合起来的人。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. Why are many people worried about AI and jobs?
2. What example shows that technology creates more jobs than it destroys?
3. What should young people do first, according to the passage?
4. What "human skills" does the passage say machines find hard?
5. What does the last sentence "Prepare for it" suggest?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. Because they fear robots will take their jobs.
2. When cars appeared, horse-carriage drivers lost jobs, but new jobs appeared in driving, road building, and car repair.
3. Learn to work with technology; use AI as a tool.
4. Creativity, communication, and caring for others.
5. Do not fear the future; develop skills and keep learning to be ready for change.
