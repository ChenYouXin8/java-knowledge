# 精读 08 · Coffee: A Cup of History（咖啡：一杯历史）

> 主题：咖啡文化与历史 | 难度：四级 | 约 210 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Every morning, millions of people start their day with a cup of coffee. But where does this drink come from? The story begins in Africa, more than a thousand years ago.

According to one old story, a goat herder in Ethiopia noticed that his goats became very active after eating red berries from a certain tree. He tried the berries himself and felt more awake. People soon learned to make a drink from them, and coffee was born.

Coffee traveled slowly around the world. It reached the Middle East, then Europe, and later the Americas. In the 1600s, coffee houses opened in cities like London and Paris. People went there not only to drink coffee but also to talk, read newspapers, and share ideas. Some people even called them "schools of wisdom".

Today, coffee is a huge business. People drink it for the taste, for the energy, or for the social time with friends. But too much coffee can also cause problems. Drinking too much can make you feel worried or keep you from sleeping well.

So how much is enough? Most doctors say that three or four cups a day is fine for healthy adults. Remember to enjoy your coffee, but listen to your body. A good cup can make your day better, but a good night's sleep is still the best.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> According to one old story, a goat herder in Ethiopia noticed that his goats became very active after eating red berries from a certain tree.

- 主干：a goat herder **noticed** that...（主 + 谓 + 宾语从句）
- 修饰：`According to one old story` 是状语；从句主干是 `his goats became active`；`after eating red berries` 是时间状语
- 译文：据一个古老的故事说，埃塞俄比亚的一位牧羊人注意到，他的山羊在吃了某种树上的红色浆果后变得非常活跃。

**句 2：**
> People went there not only to drink coffee but also to talk, read newspapers, and share ideas.

- 主干：People **went** there（主 + 谓 + 状）
- 修饰：`not only to drink coffee but also to talk, read newspapers, and share ideas` 是并列的目的状语（不仅……而且……）
- 译文：人们去那里不仅为了喝咖啡，也为了聊天、读报和交流想法。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. Where does the story of coffee begin?
2. How was coffee discovered, according to the old story?
3. Why were coffee houses called "schools of wisdom"?
4. What problems can too much coffee cause?
5. How many cups of coffee a day is fine for healthy adults?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. In Africa, more than a thousand years ago (Ethiopia).
2. A goat herder noticed his goats became active after eating red berries; he tried them and felt more awake.
3. Because people went there to talk, read newspapers, and share ideas.
4. It can make you feel worried or keep you from sleeping well.
5. Three or four cups a day is fine for healthy adults.

## 五、Summary 练习（第 3 个月阶段用，现在可以先看一眼）

**公式：谁 + 做了什么 + 结果/影响**
