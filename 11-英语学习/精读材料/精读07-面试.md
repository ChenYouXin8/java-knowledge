# 精读 07 · Prepare for the Interview（为面试做准备）

> 主题：求职与面试 | 难度：四级 | 约 220 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Li is a student who is looking for an internship. Last month, he had his first job interview. He was nervous, and he talked too fast. He did not get the offer. But he did not give up. He decided to prepare better next time.

Good preparation starts before the interview. First, learn about the company. What do they do? What are their products? This helps you ask smart questions. Second, review your projects. Be ready to explain what you did, why you did it, and what problems you met. Third, practice answering common questions out loud. Speaking with a friend or even to yourself makes you more confident.

During the interview, remember to slow down. It is normal to feel nervous. Take a deep breath and speak clearly. You do not have to answer immediately; a few seconds of thinking shows you are careful, not slow.

Do not forget the end. Ask one or two questions about the job or the team. This shows real interest. And send a short thank-you message after the interview. It is a small step, but it can leave a good impression.

An interview is not a test you pass or fail. It is a conversation. The company wants to know you, and you want to know them. With good preparation, you can walk in with confidence.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> Be ready to explain what you did, why you did it, and what problems you met.

- 主干：Be **ready** to explain...（祈使句，省略主语 You）
- 修饰：`what you did`、`why you did it`、`what problems you met` 是三个并列的**宾语从句**，作 explain 的宾语
- 译文：准备好解释你做了什么、为什么这么做、遇到了什么问题。

**句 2：**
> You do not have to answer immediately; a few seconds of thinking shows you are careful, not slow.

- 主干：You do not **have** to answer immediately（分号连接两个独立分句）；第二分句主干是 `a few seconds of thinking **shows** you are careful`
- 修饰：第二分句中 `you are careful, not slow` 是 **宾语从句**；分号相当于"因为/说明"
- 译文：你不必立刻回答；几秒钟的思考说明你是仔细，而不是迟钝。
