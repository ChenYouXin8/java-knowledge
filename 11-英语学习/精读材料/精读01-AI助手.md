# 精读 01 · A Helper That Never Sleeps（永不休息的助手）

> 主题：AI 助手与生活 | 难度：四级 | 约 220 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「英语学习三个月重启计划」第一阶段的**精读五步法**完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Meet Xiao Ming, a college student who is learning to code. Every night, he talks to an AI assistant on his phone. He asks it questions about programming, and it answers in seconds. "It is like having a patient teacher," he says. The AI never gets tired, never loses its temper, and always explains things again if he does not understand.

AI assistants are becoming part of daily life. They can write emails, translate languages, plan trips, and even help doctors read medical reports. Many companies use them to serve customers day and night. In schools, students use them to check homework and practice speaking English.

However, the AI is not perfect. Sometimes it gives wrong answers with great confidence. Teachers worry that students may depend on it too much and stop thinking by themselves. There are also questions about privacy: what happens to the data we share with these tools?

Experts say the key is to use AI wisely. It is a tool, not a replacement for our own thinking. The best users are those who ask good questions, check the answers, and learn from them. Just like learning to ride a bicycle, the AI can support you at the beginning, but in the end, you must ride on your own.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> Teachers worry that students may depend on it too much and stop thinking by themselves.

- 主干：Teachers **worry**（主 + 谓）
- 修饰：`that...` 是 **宾语从句**，作 worry 的宾语；从句里 and 连接两个并列谓语 `may depend on it too much` 和 `stop thinking`
- 译文：老师们担心，学生可能会过度依赖它，不再自己思考。

**句 2：**
> Just like learning to ride a bicycle, the AI can support you at the beginning, but in the end, you must ride on your own.

- 主干：`the AI can support you` **but** `you must ride on your own`（两个并列分句）
- 修饰：句首 `Just like learning to ride a bicycle` 是**方式状语**，用打比方说明
- 译文：就像学骑自行车一样，AI 能在开始时扶你一把，但最后你必须自己骑。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. Why does Xiao Ming say the AI is like a patient teacher?
2. What are three uses of AI assistants mentioned in the passage?
3. What worries do teachers have about AI?
4. According to experts, how should we use AI wisely?
5. What does the bicycle metaphor（比喻）mean in the last sentence?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. Because it never gets tired, never loses its temper, and always explains again.
2. Writing emails, translating languages, planning trips / helping doctors read reports / checking homework.
3. Students may depend on it too much and stop thinking by themselves.
4. Use it as a tool: ask good questions, check the answers, learn from them.
5. AI can help at the start, but in the end we must think and act on our own.
