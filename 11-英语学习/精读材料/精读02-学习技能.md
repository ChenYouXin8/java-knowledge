# 精读 02 · Practice Makes Perfect（熟能生巧）

> 主题：如何有效学习技能 | 难度：四级 | 约 215 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Everyone knows the old saying: practice makes perfect. But is it really true? Not all practice is useful. If you practice the same thing in the same way every day, you may only become better at repeating, not at improving.

Experts call the best way "deliberate practice". This means practicing with a clear goal and getting feedback. For example, when you learn to play the guitar, you do not just play your favorite song again and again. You focus on the hardest part, slow down, and correct your mistakes one by one.

Deliberate practice is often difficult and even boring. It asks you to leave your comfort zone and try things that you cannot do yet. That is why many people stop early. They enjoy the easy part and avoid the hard part.

The good news is that the brain can change. Every time you practice something difficult, your brain builds new connections. This is called neuroplasticity. It means that with the right method and enough time, almost anyone can improve at almost anything.

So the next time you want to learn something new, do not just practice more. Practice better. Set a small goal, get feedback, and challenge yourself just a little. Remember: practice does not make perfect. Perfect practice makes perfect.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> Every time you practice something difficult, your brain builds new connections.

- 主干：your brain **builds** new connections（主 + 谓 + 宾）
- 修饰：`Every time you practice something difficult` 是**时间状语从句**，表示"每当……的时候"
- 译文：每当你练习一件有难度的事，大脑就会建立新的连接。

**句 2：**
> It means that with the right method and enough time, almost anyone can improve at almost anything.

- 主干：It **means** that...（主 + 谓 + 宾语从句）
- 修饰：从句主干是 `almost anyone can improve`；`with the right method and enough time` 是方式状语
- 译文：这意味着，只要有正确的方法和足够的时间，几乎任何人都能在几乎任何事情上取得进步。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. Why is not all practice useful, according to the passage?
2. What is "deliberate practice"? Give one example from the passage.
3. Why do many people stop practicing early?
4. What does "neuroplasticity" mean?
5. What is the difference between "practice makes perfect" and "perfect practice makes perfect"?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. Because practicing the same thing in the same way only makes you better at repeating, not at improving.
2. Practicing with a clear goal and getting feedback. Example: focus on the hardest part of a song, slow down, correct mistakes one by one.
3. Because deliberate practice is difficult and boring; it asks you to leave your comfort zone.
4. The brain can change and build new connections through practice.
5. The first only means more practice; the second means practicing in the right way (with goals and feedback).
