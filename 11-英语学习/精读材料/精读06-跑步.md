# 精读 06 · Run for Your Health（为健康而跑）

> 主题：跑步与健身 | 难度：四级 | 约 215 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Chen started running three months ago. At first, he could only run for ten minutes before feeling tired. Now he runs five kilometers three times a week. He says he feels stronger, sleeps better, and thinks more clearly.

Running is one of the easiest sports to start. You do not need a gym or expensive equipment. A pair of good shoes and a safe place are enough. You can run in a park, around your school, or even in your neighborhood.

If you are new to running, remember three rules. First, start slowly. Begin with walking, and add running little by little. Second, be regular. Running for 30 minutes three times a week is better than running two hours once a week. Third, listen to your body. If you feel pain in your knees or ankles, stop and rest. Do not run through pain.

Running also helps your mind. Many runners say they feel calmer after a run. The exercise releases chemicals in the brain that make you feel good. It can help you deal with stress and study better.

You do not have to run fast or run far. The most important thing is to keep going. Every run, even a short one, is a step toward a healthier you.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> Running for 30 minutes three times a week is better than running two hours once a week.

- 主干：Running for 30 minutes three times a week **is** better than...（动名词短语作主语）
- 修饰：`than running two hours once a week` 是比较结构；主语和比较对象都是动名词短语
- 译文：每周跑三次、每次 30 分钟，比每周跑一次、跑两小时更好。

**句 2：**
> The exercise releases chemicals in the brain that make you feel good.

- 主干：The exercise **releases** chemicals（主 + 谓 + 宾）
- 修饰：`in the brain` 是地点状语；`that make you feel good` 是**定语从句**修饰 chemicals（让你感觉良好的化学物质）
- 译文：运动会释放大脑中让你感觉良好的化学物质。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. What changes did Chen see after three months of running?
2. What do you need to start running?
3. What are the three rules for new runners?
4. Why is "30 minutes three times a week" better than "two hours once a week"?
5. How does running help your mind?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. He feels stronger, sleeps better, and thinks more clearly.
2. A pair of good shoes and a safe place; no gym or expensive equipment needed.
3. Start slowly; be regular; listen to your body (stop if you feel pain).
4. Because being regular is more important than running a lot at once.
5. It releases chemicals that make you feel good and helps you deal with stress.

## 五、Summary 练习（第 3 个月阶段用，现在可以先看一眼）

**公式：谁 + 做了什么 + 结果/影响**

参考答案（3-5 句）：

New runners should start slowly, run regularly, and listen to their bodies. Running also helps the mind by releasing chemicals that reduce stress.
