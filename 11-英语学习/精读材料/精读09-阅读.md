# 精读 09 · Read More, Worry Less（多读书，少焦虑）

> 主题：阅读的价值 | 难度：四级 | 约 215 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Wang used to spend his evenings scrolling through his phone. Videos, messages, and short news filled his free time. But at the end of the day, he felt empty and tired. One day, he decided to try something old: reading a real book.

At first, it was hard to sit still for ten minutes. His mind kept wanting to check the phone. But after a few weeks, something changed. He could focus longer, and he felt calmer. Reading became his favorite way to rest.

Reading has many hidden benefits. It gives your brain a break from fast-moving screens. It teaches you new words without trying. It also helps you understand other people, because stories let you see the world through another person's eyes.

Reading does not mean you must read famous books. Start with anything you enjoy: sports news, short stories, or a book about your hobby. The rule is simple: read what you like, and read a little every day. Ten pages a day is better than a hundred pages once a month.

Some people say they have no time to read. But think about it: we all find time for the things we truly want. Put your phone in another room, open a book, and give yourself twenty quiet minutes. Your mind will thank you.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> It also helps you understand other people, because stories let you see the world through another person's eyes.

- 主干：It also **helps** you understand other people（主 + 谓 + 宾 + 宾补）
- 修饰：`because stories let you see the world through another person's eyes` 是**原因状语从句**；从句主干是 `stories let you see the world`
- 译文：它还能帮你理解他人，因为故事让你透过另一个人的眼睛看世界。

**句 2：**
> Ten pages a day is better than a hundred pages once a month.

- 主干：Ten pages a day **is** better than...（主 + 系 + 表 + 比较）
- 用法：把"每天十页"和"每月一百页"作比较，强调**规律性**比总量更重要
- 译文：每天读十页，胜过每月一口气读一百页。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. How did Wang feel at the end of each day before he started reading?
2. What changed after Wang read for a few weeks?
3. What are three hidden benefits of reading?
4. What kind of books should you start reading?
5. What is the "rule" for reading in this passage?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. He felt empty and tired.
2. He could focus longer and felt calmer.
3. It gives the brain a break from screens; teaches new words; helps you understand others (any three).
4. Anything you enjoy: sports news, short stories, or a book about your hobby.
5. Read what you like, and read a little every day (ten pages a day is better than a hundred once a month).
