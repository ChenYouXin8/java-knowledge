# 精读 05 · Home Cooking（在家做饭）

> 主题：健康生活与烹饪 | 难度：四级 | 约 215 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Ordering takeout is fast and easy. But more and more young people are learning to cook at home. Why? The first reason is health. When you cook, you control the oil, salt, and sugar in your food. Restaurant food often has more of these than you think.

The second reason is money. Cooking at home is usually much cheaper than eating out. A meal that costs 30 yuan in a restaurant may only cost 10 yuan when you make it yourself.

Many people think cooking is hard, but it does not have to be. Start with simple dishes: fried eggs, tomato soup, or rice with vegetables. Follow the steps, and soon you will feel more confident. You do not need many tools, either. A pan, a pot, and a good knife are enough for most beginners.

Cooking also brings joy. It is a way to relax after work and a way to take care of yourself. When you finish a dish and it smells good, you feel a small sense of pride.

So next time you are about to order takeout, think again. Try cooking one simple meal this week. Your body and your wallet will both say thank you.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> A meal that costs 30 yuan in a restaurant may only cost 10 yuan when you make it yourself.

- 主干：A meal **may** only **cost** 10 yuan（主 + 谓 + 宾）
- 修饰：`that costs 30 yuan in a restaurant` 是**定语从句**修饰 a meal；`when you make it yourself` 是**时间状语从句**
- 译文：在餐厅要花 30 元的一顿饭，自己做可能只需要 10 元。

**句 2：**
> It is a way to relax after work and a way to take care of yourself.

- 主干：It **is** a way... and a way...（主 + 系 + 表，两个并列表语）
- 修饰：`to relax after work` 和 `to take care of yourself` 都是**不定式**作后置定语，修饰 way
- 译文：它是一种下班后放松的方式，也是一种照顾自己的方式。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. What are the two main reasons young people cook at home?
2. Why is restaurant food not always healthy, according to the passage?
3. What should a beginner start cooking with?
4. What tools does a beginner need?
5. Besides health and money, what is another benefit of cooking?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. Health (control oil, salt, sugar) and money (cheaper than eating out).
2. Because it often has more oil, salt, and sugar than you think.
3. Simple dishes like fried eggs, tomato soup, or rice with vegetables.
4. A pan, a pot, and a good knife are enough.
5. It brings joy / it is a way to relax and take care of yourself.
