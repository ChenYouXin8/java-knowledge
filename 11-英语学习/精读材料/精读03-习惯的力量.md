# 精读 03 · The Power of Good Habits（好习惯的力量）

> 主题：习惯养成与自我管理 | 难度：四级 | 约 210 词 | 生词率约 10%
> 使用方法：按「精读五步法」完成（通读 → 细读 → 查词 → 拆句 → 朗读）

---

## 一、原文阅读（先不查词，限时 4 分钟通读一遍）

Have you ever made a plan to exercise more or study harder, only to give up after a few days? Do not be too hard on yourself. The problem is not your willpower. The problem is that you are trying to change with willpower alone, and willpower runs out quickly.

Scientists who study behavior say that habits are the key to lasting change. A habit is something you do automatically, without thinking too much. Brushing your teeth, for example, is a habit. You do not decide to do it every morning; you just do it.

How do you build a good habit? First, start very small. If you want to read more, start with just two pages a day. Small steps are easy to keep. Second, attach the new habit to an old one. After you finish your breakfast, read two pages. The old habit becomes a reminder. Third, be patient. On average, it takes about 66 days to form a new habit.

There is one more secret: do not miss twice. If you skip one day, that is fine. But do not skip two days in a row, because that often breaks the habit completely.

Remember, you do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems. Build good systems, and good habits will follow.

## 三、长难句拆解（每天拆 2 句，先把主干找出来）

**句 1：**
> Scientists who study behavior say that habits are the key to lasting change.

- 主干：Scientists **say** that...（主 + 谓 + 宾语从句）
- 修饰：`who study behavior` 是**定语从句**修饰 Scientists（研究行为的科学家们）
- 译文：研究行为科学的科学家们说，习惯是持久改变的关键。

**句 2：**
> You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.

- 主干：You do not **rise** to the level of your goals / You **fall** to the level of your systems（两个并列句）
- 用法：rise 与 fall 对仗，表达"你并不会达到目标的高度，只会落到系统（方法体系）的高度"
- 译文：你不会上升到目标的高度，你只会掉到你方法体系的高度。

## 四、理解练习（读完合上文章，试着回答）

1. What is the real problem when people give up on their plans?
2. Why is brushing teeth used as an example of a habit?
3. What are the three steps to build a good habit?
4. What is the "one more secret" about habits?
5. What does the last sentence "You fall to the level of your systems" mean?

**参考要点**（先自己答，再看这里）
1. They are trying to change with willpower alone, and willpower runs out quickly.
2. Because it is done automatically without thinking.
3. Start very small; attach the new habit to an old one; be patient (about 66 days).
4. Do not miss twice in a row, because that often breaks the habit completely.
5. Good results come from good systems/methods, not just from high goals.

## 五、Summary 练习（第 3 个月阶段用，现在可以先看一眼）

**公式：谁 + 做了什么 + 结果/影响**

参考答案（3-5 句）：
> Willpower alone is not enough for lasting change; habits are the key. To build good habits, start small, connect the new habit to an old one, and be patient. It takes about 66 days on average. Also, do not miss two days in a row. In the end, good systems create good habits and good results.
